Who We Are

Pune's most
active animal rescue

Founded in 2012, ARC Pune — Animal Rescue Committee — is a volunteer-driven organization dedicated to rescuing and rehabilitating injured, sick, and abandoned animals across Pune district.

We run on compassion and community. From emergency street rescues at 2 AM to organizing adoption drives, our 300+ volunteers work tirelessly every single day. No animal is left behind.
